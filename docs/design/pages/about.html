The mission

What separates leaders who succeed fastest from everyone else is rarely intelligence. It is access to the right thinking tools at the right moment.

My mission: put these tools in the hands of every leader — not just the ones in business schools with expensive advisors and curated networks. The first-generation entrepreneur. The student who does not yet know they will lead something important. The manager who stepped up before they felt ready.

The most dangerous gap in modern leadership is not a gap in ambition. It is a gap in tools. This book closes that gap.
